Architecture

Confluent Platform runs as a workload on Streamtime.

Your Confluent subscription stays as it is. Streamtime deploys Confluent Platform, including Confluent Flink, through its Confluent provider on your license, and operates it alongside the rest of your fleet. Kubernetes deployments use Confluent for Kubernetes (CFK). VM deployments use cp-ansible and are in limited preview.

Air-gapped ready

Connected, restricted or fully air-gapped.

Connected

The management plane reaches your clusters through a secure tunnel opened from your side. No inbound ports.

Restricted egress

Private overlays and allow-listed egress for regulated networks, with the same APIs and workflows.

Air-gapped

The whole management plane runs inside your boundary, with local registries, offline bundles and self-healing control planes.
