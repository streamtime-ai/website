--- title: FAQ heading: Frequently asked questions description: Common questions about migration, infrastructure, commercial distributions, air-gapped deployments and operations. ---
Do we have to replatform to use Streamtime?
No. Streamtime can support your existing Kafka clusters where they run today, whether they are managed, self-hosted or on another distribution. When you are ready to move, migration is part of the engagement. The pricing page lists the support plans.
Where does Streamtime run?
On the major public clouds, on Kubernetes or OpenShift clusters you already operate, in your data center and at the edge. The supported providers and cluster requirements are on the home page and in the docs.
Which workloads are supported?
Streaming, stream processing and real-time analytics. The workloads section on the home page lists the engines in each category.
How do commercial Kafka distributions work?
Streamtime has providers for commercial distributions such as Confluent Platform. A provider handles deployment, configuration, upgrades and recovery for that distribution, so it runs on the same fleet, identity and operations model as Apache Kafka. Licensing is bring your own: you keep your subscription with the vendor, and Streamtime deploys and supports the distribution under that license. See Confluent Platform with Streamtime.
How is Confluent Platform deployed?
On Kubernetes, Streamtime deploys Confluent Platform with Confluent for Kubernetes (CFK), covering Kafka, Schema Registry, Connect, Control Center and Confluent Flink. Deployment on VMs uses cp-ansible; that support is limited and in preview.
Can Streamtime run in air-gapped environments?
Yes. Connected, restricted-egress and fully air-gapped deployments use the same APIs, operators and workflows. In an air-gap, the whole management plane runs inside your boundary with local registries, offline bundles and self-healing control planes.
How does support work in an air-gapped environment?
Local services self-heal against known failure modes, and tested fixes ship as offline bundles. Support engagements go through auditable, policy-controlled artifact exchanges instead of a live tunnel into your enclave.
Can AI agents operate the platform?
Yes. The Streamtime MCP server gives any MCP-capable agent the fleet's desired and actual state, metrics, recent changes and in-flight operations. The Streamtime AI assistant adds an operations harness with guarded, verifiable actions.
How is capacity sized?
Clusters are benchmarked on your hardware for sustained throughput, latency and failure headroom, then checked against the workload's declared requirements before they go into production.
